About me

I'm a virtual assistant who supports businesses and professionals with day-to-day administration, customer communication and workflow automation.

I'm a Social Work graduate of the University of Lagos, and my training shows in how I work: I listen carefully, keep accurate records and handle sensitive information with discretion.
